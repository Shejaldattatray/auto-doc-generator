### `add(a, b)`

*   **Purpose:** Adds two numbers together. The `+ 0` in the return statement is redundant but does not affect the arithmetic sum.
*   **Parameters:**
    *   `a` (int or float): The first number to be added.
    *   `b` (int or float): The second number to be added.
*   **Returns:**
    *   (int or float): The sum of `a` and `b`.

---

### `multiply(a, b)`

*   **Purpose:** Multiplies two numbers.
*   **Parameters:**
    *   `a` (int or float): The first number (multiplicand).
    *   `b` (int or float): The second number (multiplier).
*   **Returns:**
    *   (int or float): The product of `a` and `b`.
